01 / EDUCATIONSCHOOL WEBSITE & DASHBOARD

Stans Hillside School Website

A web-based school management system that integrates public school information with administrative and academic tools, connecting administrators, teachers, students and parents through a centralised platform.

What you’ll find in the project

  • Admin Dashboard: Manages school operations, user accounts, and administrative records.
  • Teacher Dashboard: Enables teachers to manage classes, student attendance, and academic performance.
  • Student Dashboard: Allows students to access timetables, assignments, announcements, examination results and academic progress.
  • Parent Dashboard: Enables parents to monitor their children's and access school updates.
  • Secure, role-based user authentication. Responsive design for desktop, tablet and mobile devices..

02 / APPLICATIONWEATHER DASHBOARD

Weather App

A clean weather dashboard for checking local conditions or searching for another city. It keeps the most useful forecast details together so users can understand the weather at a glance.

What you’ll find in the project

  • City or town search to look up weather conditions for a chosen location.
  • Current temperature and weather conditions presented in a dashboard layout.
  • Forecast information, including upcoming days and helpful weather indicators.
  • A Celsius/Fahrenheit switch for the preferred temperature unit.
  • A responsive interface that keeps the forecast readable on different screen sizes.
